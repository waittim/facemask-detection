How to Cite WearMask

Preferred citation formats for the WearMask research paper and software.

Research paper (preferred)

When discussing the WearMask system, methods, or research contributions, cite the peer-reviewed paper:

Wang, Z., Wang, P., Louis, P. C., Wheless, L. E., & Huo, Y. (2023). WearMask: Fast in-browser face mask detection with serverless edge computing for COVID-19. Electronic Imaging, 35(11). https://doi.org/10.2352/EI.2023.35.11.HPCI-229

BibTeX

@article{
    wang_wang_louis_wheless_huo_2023,
    title={WearMask: Fast in-browser face mask detection with serverless edge computing for covid-19},
    volume={35},
    DOI={10.2352/ei.2023.35.11.hpci-229},
    number={11},
    journal={Electronic Imaging},
    author={Wang, Zekun and Wang, Pengwei and Louis, Peter C. and Wheless, Lee E. and Huo, Yuankai},
    year={2023}
}

Software repositories

When reusing or discussing implementation code, also reference the relevant repository:

  • Browser deployment: github.com/waittim/facemask-detection
  • Training and data: github.com/waittim/mask-detector

Prefer citing a specific release or commit hash rather than a moving master branch when reproducibility matters.
