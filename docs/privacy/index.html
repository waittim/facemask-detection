Privacy & Limitations

How WearMask handles camera data, user privacy commitments, and known system boundaries.

100% Client-Side Privacy Model

WearMask performs all face mask detection entirely inside your web browser using WebAssembly. Camera frames are processed locally on your device. No images, video, or detection results are uploaded to a WearMask application server.

What is not collected

Zero image storage Camera images or video are never stored or uploaded remotely.
No backend tracking Detection results are never transmitted to backend databases.
No account required No registration, sign-in, or personal identification is needed.

Offline Operation

After the WebAssembly model files are loaded, detection can continue even if you disconnect from the internet. The initial page load requires network access to download the application and model assets.

System Limitations & Scope

Non-Medical Device Disclaimer: WearMask is a research prototype and public demonstration project. It is not a medical device or diagnostic tool and must not be used for medical diagnosis or safety enforcement.

Detection Accuracy & Edge Cases

  • Detection performance depends on camera resolution, lighting, and frame rates.
  • Low light, heavy shadows, or strong backlighting may reduce accuracy.
  • Extreme side angles, small faces, or heavy physical occlusions may cause missed detections.
  • The model evaluates visual mask presence, not medical filter rating or seal tightness.

Browser & Hardware Dependencies

  • Inference speed varies across devices depending on CPU capabilities.
  • Modern Chrome with WebAssembly SIMD and multi-threading provides optimal performance.
  • iOS Safari is supported but may yield lower frame rates on older devices.
