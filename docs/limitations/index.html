Known Limitations

Important boundaries and constraints of the WearMask system.

Not a medical device. WearMask is a research prototype and public demonstration. It must not be used for medical diagnosis, clinical decision-making, or regulatory compliance without independent validation.

Detection accuracy

  • Performance depends on camera quality, resolution, and frame rate.
  • Poor lighting, backlighting, or heavy shadows reduce accuracy.
  • Small faces, extreme poses, or partial occlusion may be missed or misclassified.
  • The model detects visual mask-wearing patterns, not medical effectiveness or fit.

Browser and hardware

  • Frame rate varies significantly across CPUs, browsers, and devices.
  • Best performance requires modern Chrome with WebAssembly SIMD and threads support.
  • Safari on iOS is supported but typically runs at a lower frame rate.
  • Older browsers without WebAssembly support cannot run the demo.

Scope of use

  • Designed for research, education, and demonstration purposes.
  • Not validated for high-stakes public health enforcement.
  • Does not verify mask type (N95, surgical, cloth) or proper coverage beyond visual detection.
  • May produce false positives or false negatives in real-world conditions.

Model and data

  • Trained on a specific dataset; generalization to all populations and environments is not guaranteed.
  • Model weights and architecture reflect 2020–2023 research; no continuous retraining is provided.
