Setup How to get the best experience?
  • Launch the latest Chrome browser.
  • Go to chrome://flags and enable WebAssembly features.
  • Restart Chrome, open this page, and allow camera access.
  • For iOS, use Safari (frame rate may be lower).

Frame rate depends on your device CPU. Higher performance chips deliver faster inference.

Privacy Privacy statement

Everything runs fully inside your browser via WebAssembly. No images or video frames leave your device, and no data is stored on any server. You can even disconnect the internet once the detector loads.

Project Project overview

WearMask is a deep learning project based on the Yolo-Fastest model, trained with PyTorch, and deployed through NCNN and WebAssembly.

Everything is open-sourced on GitHub. You can also check arXiv and my blog for more context. To contribute, send incorrect detections to wearmask.feedback@gmail.com. Thank you!
